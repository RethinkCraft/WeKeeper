# Roadmap

## 0.7.0 product boundary

- Self-hosted message dashboard, selected-group outgoing sync, alerts, avatars and private AI conversations.
- Protocol-only group bot with scoped business tools, versioned personas, local provider credentials and deliberate cloud configuration alignment.
- Shared local archive/media readers and the existing fifteen-tool archive MCP.

Existing schema 4 and 5 archives retain their version. Scheduled summaries, the UI bot, pilot mirroring and their experimental harnesses are removed.

## Next work

1. Expand deidentified protocol and cached-media regression fixtures from naturally observed cases.
2. Improve transport portability and document acceptance by exact gateway/client build.
3. Improve operational diagnostics for delayed reception, uncertain delivery and sync recovery without replaying business writes.

New product features need a concrete use case and an acceptance path. OCR, video processing, Moments, arbitrary page fetching, uncached chat-media downloading and general desktop automation remain outside this release.
